## Why this guide exists

Most people leave school thinking physics is a wall of formulas. It isn't. Physics is just **the rules the universe runs on** — and once you see _why_ a rule works, the math becomes the easy part.

This guide is written for the person who sat in class thinking, _"Wait, but why?"_ and never got a real answer. I'm going to explain things the way a mechanical engineer thinks about them: with pictures in your head, real objects you can touch, and zero shame in slowing down.

## How to read this guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read in order">
    Each page builds on the one before it. Don't skip ahead — the "aha" moments are stacked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Picture it first, math second">
    Every concept starts with a real-world picture. If you can't see it in your head, the formula won't stick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the thought experiments">
    When I say "imagine pushing a shopping cart," actually pause and imagine it. That's where understanding lives.
  </Step>
</Steps>
